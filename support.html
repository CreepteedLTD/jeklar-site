Common questions

My gym is not listed

Pick Gym session (any well-equipped gym) or Home session (no equipment), or add your gym from the picker: name, a pin on the map, and either photos for the AI to read or a hand-picked equipment list.

I own a gym and want it listed for everyone

Add it as a public gym and switch on I own this gym, with the owner's name and a proof such as an invoice or rental agreement. A reviewer approves it, usually within a day, and you can then add photos, read the equipment and publish it.

Subscriptions

Plans are bought and cancelled through the App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Bought on another device? Use Restore purchases on the Plans screen. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting my account

In the app: Account → Delete account. Everything tied to the account is removed at once.

A session looks wrong

Tap Not this — ask again on the session overview to get another, and use Station taken or Skip exercise during the workout. Tell us what was wrong; it helps.
